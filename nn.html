Noire et Neige

Created a fine jewelry brand, designed a catalog and website.

Web

Editorial

Research

This project focused on the creation of a luxury jewelry brand, beginning with extensive research into the high-end jewelry market. The designer analyzed existing luxury brands to identify key visual and strategic elements that convey sophistication and exclusivity. Visual references were collected to inform the tone, aesthetic, and positioning of the brand. The research revealed that simplicity, refinement, and attention to detail are essential qualities in the luxury jewelry space. These findings shaped the brand’s tone of voice and visual identity, forming a strong, cohesive foundation for Noir et Neige.

Concept Generation

Concept development started with exploratory logo sketches designed to reflect the brand’s elegant yet moody personality. Once the visual identity began to take form, the focus shifted to editorial design, where high-contrast layouts and striking jewelry-centric imagery were used to communicate visual drama and sophistication. A grayscale palette accented with gold and silver was selected to reinforce the brand’s name Noir et Neige, meaning black and snow, and to convey a refined yet bold aesthetic. The final outcome positions Noir et Neige as a contemporary luxury brand, characterized by elegance, contrast, and timeless appeal.
