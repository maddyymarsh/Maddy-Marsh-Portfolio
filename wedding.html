Wedding Collateral

Created a wedding package including a save the date, official invitation, RSVP card, menu and envelope.

Research

This project required the creation of a cohesive set of wedding collateral for an event taking place in Paros, Greece. Research focused on the location’s cultural and visual influences, as well as common design approaches in high-end wedding stationery. Findings revealed that traditional Greek color palettes, primarily blues and whites, paired beautifully with the soft pink tones found in local flora. Additionally, elegant typefaces with a hand-written or calligraphic feel were common in modern wedding design, evoking a sense of romance and personalization.

Concept Generation

The design process began with hand-drawn sketches for each item in the stationery suite. Once the creative direction was established, digital mockups were developed in Adobe Illustrator. Several typographic combinations were tested to strike the right balance of elegance and modernity. The final designs incorporate a refined color palette and stylistic elements that reflect both the geographic location and the celebratory nature of the event, resulting in a set of timeless and personalized wedding materials.
