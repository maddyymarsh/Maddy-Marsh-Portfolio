The Myth of Sisyphus Book Jacket Redesign

Designed book jacket cover for "The Myth of Sisyphus".

This project involved designing visual materials inspired by the themes and tone of The Myth of Sisyphus. Research began with a deep dive into the book’s philosophical message and emotional weight. Special attention was given to visual metaphors associated with repetition, struggle, and existentialism, key motifs within the text.
