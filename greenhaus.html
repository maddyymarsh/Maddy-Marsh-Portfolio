Green Haus

Created a 9x12 coffee table book about caring for house plants.

Research

This project involved designing a coffee table book centered on the topic of indoor plants. Research focused on understanding the different categories of indoor plants, their defining characteristics, and proper care methods. This foundation informed design decisions that aligned with the subject matter, ensuring both accuracy and relevance. Additional research examined the structure and visual appeal of contemporary coffee table books, emphasizing principles of clarity, simplicity, and aesthetic balance. Findings highlighted the effectiveness of clean layouts and generous spacing to maintain an engaging yet unobtrusive visual experience for the reader.

Concept Generation

Guided by the research, the concept phase began with defining a cohesive visual direction. The chosen aesthetic embraced minimalism infused with Bauhaus-inspired elements, establishing a modern and structured framework. This approach prioritized strong imagery and concise information, allowing key visuals and content to stand out while maintaining an overall sense of balance and refinement.
