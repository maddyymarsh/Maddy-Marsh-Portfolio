Misfits Album Cover Redesign

Created a cover for "The Devil's Rain" album with a strong emphasis on typography and type to create a mood and visual look.

This project involved rebranding an album by the punk band Misfits, with a strong emphasis on typographic expression. The design process began with research into the band’s legacy, visual identity, and the broader punk music aesthetic. The findings emphasized the use of dark color schemes, bold typography, and gritty, rebellious design elements, hallmarks of the genre and the band’s brand.
