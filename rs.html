Rolling Stone Redesign

Designed a 12-page editorial with ads following Rolling Stone Magazine's media-kit.

Software Used: Adobe InDesign, Adobe Photoshop.

Research

This project tasked the designer with developing a magazine concept aligned with the Rolling Stone brand, guided by its official media kit. Initial research involved analyzing the brand’s design language, including color palettes, typography, and layout conventions. These elements provided a clear understanding of the editorial voice and visual style required to maintain consistency with Rolling Stone’s established identity while exploring creative new directions.

Concept Generation

With a well-defined visual direction in place, concept development began through hand-drawn layout sketches tailored to the specific dimensions of the selected content and imagery. The final design carefully aligns with the Rolling Stone brand, while also bringing a fresh, focused perspective to a new editorial topic, effectively merging brand fidelity with originality.
