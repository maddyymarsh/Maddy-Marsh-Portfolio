Worcester Railers

Created an instagram post as well as in game updates for a Worcester Railers Game in support of Jake Thibeault.

Research

The Worcester Railers requested a custom game day graphic and an Instagram Story template for live game updates. The project began with research into visual trends used by other professional sports teams, as well as an analysis of the Railers’ existing branding and social media content. Reviewing their media kit and recent posts provided key insights into how best to visually represent the team’s identity and maintain consistency across platforms.

Concept Generation

After collecting assets and establishing a creative direction, design concepts were developed that balanced bold, energetic visuals with clear, structured layouts. The final graphics are optimized for quick readability and visual impact, enhancing the team’s digital presence and fan engagement during game days
