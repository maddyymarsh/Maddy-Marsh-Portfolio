Friends of the Plymouth Pound Website

Redesigned a multi-page website for the Friends of the Plymouth Pound animal shelter.

Languages Used: HTML, CSS.

Research

This project focused on refreshing the branding for Friends of the Plymouth Pound, a local animal shelter. Research involved a close analysis of the organization’s existing branding as well as contemporary design trends used by similar nonprofit and animal-focused organizations. Findings highlighted the emotional effectiveness of warm, inviting color palettes and impactful animal imagery in engaging audiences. These insights informed a more approachable and visually empathetic design direction, specifically addressing the original branding’s cooler tones and lack of emotional resonance.

Concept Generation

Following the research phase, design concepts were developed using Adobe Illustrator. The existing logo was subtly refined to maintain brand recognition while introducing a more modern and friendly aesthetic. Typography was carefully considered, pairing the approachable Brevia typeface with the clean and dependable Neue Haas Grotesk Text Pro to balance warmth and professionalism. The final website and brand refresh present a welcoming, user-friendly identity that supports the shelter’s mission and encourages community engagement.
