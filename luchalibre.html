Lucha Libre

Designed an 18x24 poster, 3 Instagram posts, and a t-shirt for a Lucha Libre event.

Research

The design process began with comprehensive research and the development of a moodboard to establish a strong visual and cultural foundation. Given that Lucha Libre is a niche subject, the initial focus was to immerse in its traditions, energy, and visual identity. Research revealed that Lucha Libre is a vibrant form of professional wrestling, defined by high-energy performances, theatrical storytelling, and elaborate costumes. The colorful masks worn by luchadores emerged as a central visual and symbolic element, representing character, and legacy. These insights directly informed the visual direction, guiding the creation of a design that authentically captures the dynamic spirit of Lucha Libre.

Concept Generation

Building on the research phase, the concept development process began with hand-drawn sketches and iterative visual exploration. The luchador masks, rich in cultural symbolism and visual impact, became the core design motif. Various compositions were tested using mask elements as graphic focal points and background textures. This exploration resulted in a bold and expressive poster series, designed to reflect the intensity, color, and theatrical essence of Lucha Libre. The final visuals aim to evoke the energy and storytelling tradition that define this unique cultural spectacle.
