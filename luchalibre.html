Lucha Libre

Designed an 18x24 poster, 3 Instagram posts, and a t-shirt for a Lucha Libre event.

Software Used: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop.

Research

I always start my process with research, preparing a creative brief and developing a moodboard. Going into this project, I didn’t have much knowledge on Lucha Libre events so that’s where I began. Throughout my time researching, I learned that these are lively wrestling events filled with action and storytelling between two luchadores. The costumes of the competitors is a significant element in these events, often detailing colorful and expressive masks. My research on Lucha Libre allowed me to incorporate elements in the design to visually communicate an authentic representation of these events.

Concept Generation

Once I finish gathering information and resources, I move on to physical concept generation. I hand draw sketches of potential designs. For this project, I knew I wanted to incorporate the design of the face masks that the luchadores wear as they hold a lot of importance and are a good representation of the energy that these events hold. I sketched out some options of different elements of the masks in the background which gave a unique and dynamic feel to the posters.
