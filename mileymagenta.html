Miley Magenta

Created a vector portrait of Miley Cyrus.

This project investigates the relationship between shape and color in vector portrait illustration. The selected color palette was intentionally chosen to reflect the energy and personality of the subject, Miley Cyrus. Emphasis was placed on using organic forms to create visual rhythm and depth while maintaining clarity and detail within the composition.
