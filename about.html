Hi, I’m Maddy, a visual designer and developer based near Boston, MA. I have a passion for creative problem-solving and efficiency, both of which guide every stage of my design process. My curiosity and desire for deep understanding drive me to research thoroughly and truly grasp the needs of each client.

I believe great design goes far beyond aesthetics. I’m fascinated by the psychology behind visual communication and user flow, and I approach every project with empathy. Not only designing just for how something looks, but for how it feels and functions for real people.

I’m especially interested in pursuing UI/UX design integrated with front-end development, though my skill set spans many areas of visual design. I’m currently earning my Bachelor of Science in Graphic Design with a minor in Computer Science at Curry College in Milton, MA. My education has been invaluable as it provides hands-on experience, meaningful collaboration, and lasting connections with peers and mentors.

As a Graphic Design Assistant for Curry’s Visual and Performing Arts Department, I’ve had the opportunity to work closely with faculty and contribute to creative projects that support the department’s mission.

I’m excited to continue growing as a designer and developer, creating thoughtful, meaningful work.
Let’s connect, I’d love to collaborate and bring your ideas to life.
